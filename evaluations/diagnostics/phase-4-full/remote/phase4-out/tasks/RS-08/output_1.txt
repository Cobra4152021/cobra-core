- **Incident Timeline**: On 2024-02-02, truck T-109 (T-19) departed the North depot at 06:10 with manifest M-441. Dock camera notes at Central indicate the truck arrived at 11:40 the same day. The truck was redirected to Central for inspection and was not delivered to South.
- **Manifest and Intake Discrepancy**: Manifest M-441 lists 48 sealed crates of spare parts and 2 open crates of manuals. The Central intake form records 47 sealed crates and notes one seal broken in transit. The discrepancy suggests a possible loss or damage during transit.
